THE THURSDAY PACK
Drop-in lesson for learn.stratanexus.co.uk

Open index.html from this folder (assets/ must sit next to it).
Or host the whole folder as a static lesson.

Play
  Sit down → four tiles: Offer / HMRC / Call-centre broker / Professional broker.
  Professional track: assessment → Time to Pay → week 2 cash flow → ten minutes → write the page.
  Call-centre track: sign the offer → page 11 → sweep / personal guarantee / stack → ending.

What the game teaches
  Not every broker is the same.
  A call-centre broker is found online, or rings when you are desperate.
  A professional broker is a member of the Guild of Business Finance Professionals, or is sent by a trusted business partner.
  Map the stack. Package Time to Pay. One structure.
  Strata packages. It does not lend.
