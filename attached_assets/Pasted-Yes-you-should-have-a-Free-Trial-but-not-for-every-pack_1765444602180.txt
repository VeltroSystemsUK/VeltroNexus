Yes, you should have a Free Trial, but not for every package.For a B2B app like FlowLoan, a "one-size-fits-all" trial strategy will actually hurt your sales. You need to split your approach based on the complexity of the customer.Here is the recommended "Hybrid Strategy" to balance user acquisition with high-value sales.1. The Entry-Level: "14-Day Free Trial"Target: Single Brokers (The £39/mo tier).Strategy: Low Friction (Self-Serve).Mechanism:No Credit Card Required: Just name, email, and password.Duration: 14 Days (30 days often leads to procrastination; 14 days creates urgency).The Goal: Get them hooked on the "easy wins" (e.g., adding a lead, generating a document).Why: Competitors like Brokerpad and Brickflow offer this. If you gate this behind a sales call, you will lose 80% of these smaller leads who just want to "play" with the tool on a Sunday evening.2. The Mid-Tier: "Paid Pilot" or "Concierge Trial"Target: Sales Teams (The £229/mo tier).Strategy: High Touch.Mechanism:No Open Sign-up: Do not let a manager sign up for 5 seats automatically.The Offer: "Book a Demo" → "14-Day Team Pilot."The Difference: You (or your sales process) manually set up their account, invite their 5 agents for them, and perhaps upload a few of their leads before they log in.Why: If a team manager signs up for a blank account, they have to do the work of inviting 4 people. If they get busy, they churn. If you do the setup, they are invested.3. The Lender/Enterprise: "Demo Only" (No Free Trial)Target: Lenders / Credit Teams (The £999/mo tier).Strategy: Value Sales.Mechanism: "Book a Consultation" or "Request Access."Why:Complexity: The "Credit Underwriting Module" (scorecards, committee reports) is too complex for a user to figure out alone in 10 minutes. They will get confused and think the product is "too hard."Security/IP: You don't want competitors or unqualified users snooping around your high-value IP (the underwriting logic) for free.Qualification: You need to talk to them to ensure they can actually afford £1k/month before giving them access.Summary: Your "Call to Action" (CTA) MapPackagePricingPrimary CTA ButtonSecondary LinkSolo Broker£39/moStart Free Trial (Instant access)See FeaturesSales Team£229/moBook Team DemoContact SalesLender£999/moRequest AccessView Case Studies
