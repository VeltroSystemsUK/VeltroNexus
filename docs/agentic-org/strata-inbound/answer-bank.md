No. Strata packages and structures the case and presents it to lenders. Strata does not lend, does not decide credit, and is not paid by the reader for a decision it does not make. The lender recommendation is made by Sterling, Strata's partner, once the case is built.

**Are you regulated by the FCA?**  
No. Strata arranges non-regulated commercial business-to-business finance for limited companies and is not authorised or regulated by the FCA. That is normal for this type of lending; most finance to limited companies sits outside FCA regulation. It also means the reader should expect Strata to be clear about what it does and does not do, which is why James says it plainly.

**What is a CDFI?**  
A Community Development Finance Institution: a not-for-profit lender, typically backed by the British Business Bank and regional funds, that lends to viable businesses the high street banks have declined. They are not a last resort at high cost; they are usually structured over longer terms than the short-term products the reader is currently servicing. Facilities Strata packages typically range from £25,000 to £250,000 over up to five years, subject to assessment and appetite.

**I have an HMRC Time to Pay / arrears.**  
HMRC arrangements can be settled or restructured within a consolidation where the lender's appetite allows. Being in TTP is not a bar to assessment. James asks for the current TTP terms as part of the overview.

**Will I need a personal guarantee?**  
Most commercial lending to limited companies involves a PG (a personal guarantee: the director stands behind the company debt). Whether one is required, and on what terms, is a lender decision that Shaun will explain clearly before anything is signed.

**My credit is poor / the company has CCJs.**  
Not automatically a bar. It affects appetite and structure; it is part of the assessment. James asks for it to be disclosed in the overview so nothing surprises the lender later.
