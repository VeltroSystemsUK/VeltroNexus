1. Executive Summary
This blueprint defines the operational architecture for running a commercial finance brokerage as a one-person company, powered by a workforce of purpose-built AI agents. Rather than recruiting employees to fill traditional roles, each critical business function is performed by a specialised agent that operates continuously, consistently, and at a fraction of the cost of human headcount.
The model leverages your dual advantage: deep commercial lending expertise gained from deploying over £100M in business funding, and the technical capability to build and deploy AI agents through the platform you have already created. Instead of selling this technology as a product, you will deploy it as your operating infrastructure — becoming both the proof of concept and the primary beneficiary.
Target outcome: A fully operational brokerage generating consistent deal flow, managing client relationships, and processing applications with a single human (you) making all strategic decisions and maintaining key client relationships, supported by 6–8 AI agents handling execution.
 
2. Company Structure & Operating Model
2.1 The Human-Agent Operating Model
The core principle is simple: you are the strategist, relationship holder, and decision-maker. Your agents are the workforce. Every repetitive, time-consuming, or process-driven task is delegated to an agent, leaving you free to focus on the activities that only a human with your experience can perform.
Your Role as Founder-Operator
•	Strategic direction and product decisions
•	Final approval on deals, proposals, and client-facing communications
•	Key relationship management with lenders, referral partners, and high-value clients
•	Agent performance review and optimisation
•	Regulatory compliance oversight (FCA considerations for credit broking)
Agent Roles (The Workforce)
Each agent operates within a clearly defined scope, with escalation rules that route decisions requiring judgement back to you. The agents communicate with each other through shared data layers and structured handoff protocols.
 
3. The Agent Workforce
Below is the full agent roster. The first four are your core team — the minimum viable workforce. The remaining agents add operational depth as you scale.
3.1 The Sales Agent — “Outreach”
Attribute	Detail
Mission	Generate qualified conversations with SMEs actively seeking commercial finance.
Trigger	Receives qualified leads from the Marketing Agent or prospect lists from The Prospector.
Channels	Email sequences, LinkedIn outreach, SMS follow-ups.
Escalation	Any prospect requesting a call, expressing urgency, or matching high-value criteria (£250K+).
Core Capabilities
•	Execute multi-step outreach sequences personalised to each prospect’s industry, company size, and inferred funding need
•	Respond to inbound enquiries within 2 minutes during business hours with contextually relevant replies
•	Track engagement signals (opens, clicks, replies) and adjust follow-up timing and messaging accordingly
•	Qualify prospects using BANT-style criteria adapted for commercial finance: funding amount, urgency, existing facilities, credit profile
•	Book discovery calls directly into your calendar with pre-populated briefing notes
Key Metrics
•	Outreach volume: 50–100 personalised contacts per day
•	Response rate target: 8–15% on cold outreach
•	Qualified meeting conversion: 20%+ of responses
•	Speed to lead: < 2 minutes on inbound
3.2 The Marketing Agent — “Lead Engine”
Attribute	Detail
Mission	Attract, nurture, and qualify inbound leads through content and campaigns.
Trigger	Scheduled content calendar, ad performance data, website visitor behaviour.
Channels	LinkedIn content, email newsletters, blog/SEO, paid ads (Google/LinkedIn).
Escalation	Budget decisions over £500, messaging strategy changes, brand-sensitive content.
Core Capabilities
•	Generate weekly LinkedIn posts, articles, and thought-leadership content tailored to SME finance pain points
•	Create and manage email nurture sequences for different segments: refinancing, asset finance, growth capital, cashflow
•	Monitor and optimise ad spend across Google Ads and LinkedIn, reallocating budget to best-performing campaigns
•	Score and segment leads based on engagement, company profile, and expressed intent
•	Produce monthly content calendars aligned with seasonal lending patterns (tax deadlines, year-end, Q1 planning)
•	A/B test subject lines, CTAs, and landing page copy, reporting winners weekly
Key Metrics
•	Inbound leads generated: 30–60 per month
•	Cost per qualified lead: < £25
•	Email list growth: 10% month-on-month
•	Content engagement: 3%+ LinkedIn engagement rate
3.3 The Accounts Agent — “Finance Monitor”
Attribute	Detail
Mission	Monitor cashflow, track commissions, manage invoicing, and flag financial risks.
Trigger	Deal completions, monthly cycles, invoice due dates, bank transaction feeds.
Integrations	Xero/FreeAgent API, Stripe, bank feeds, HMRC MTD.
Escalation	Overdue invoices > 30 days, cashflow projection below 60-day runway, tax filing deadlines.
Core Capabilities
•	Auto-generate invoices when deals complete, applying correct commission rates per lender agreement
•	Reconcile incoming payments against outstanding invoices and flag discrepancies
•	Produce weekly cashflow snapshots and 90-day rolling forecasts
•	Track all business expenses, categorise for tax purposes, and flag potential deductions
•	Prepare quarterly VAT submissions and year-end summaries for your accountant
•	Monitor commission clawback windows and alert you before retention periods expire
Key Metrics
•	Invoice accuracy: 99%+
•	Days sales outstanding (DSO): < 30 days average
•	Cashflow forecast accuracy: within 10% of actuals
•	Zero missed filing deadlines
3.4 The Deal Processing Agent — “Underwriter”
Attribute	Detail
Mission	Assess applications, match to lenders, prepare credit packs, and manage the deal pipeline.
Trigger	New application submission, document upload, lender response, status change.
Data Sources	Companies House, credit bureaus, bank statement OCR, filed accounts.
Escalation	Complex structures, adverse credit requiring judgement, deals over £500K, lender relationship issues.
Core Capabilities
•	Automatically pull Companies House data, verify trading status, extract filed accounts and PSC information
•	Perform initial affordability assessment using bank statement analysis and management accounts
•	Match deals against your lender panel criteria (sector, size, LTV, term, credit profile) and rank best-fit options
•	Generate professional credit packs / broker submissions formatted to each lender’s requirements
•	Track deal status across multiple lenders simultaneously, chasing for updates on defined timelines
•	Maintain a live pipeline dashboard with weighted probability and expected commission values
Key Metrics
•	Application-to-submission time: < 24 hours for standard deals
•	Lender matching accuracy: 90%+ first-choice acceptance rate
•	Pipeline visibility: real-time, weighted forecast updated daily
•	Document completeness check: 95%+ first-time pass rate with lenders
3.5 The Client Relationship Agent — “Retention”
Attribute	Detail
Mission	Maximise lifetime value through repeat business, referrals, and proactive relationship management.
Trigger	Deal completion, facility renewal dates, client milestones, seasonal check-ins.
Escalation	Client complaints, churn risk signals, high-value renewal opportunities.
Core Capabilities
•	Track all active client facilities and set automated renewal reminders 90 days before expiry
•	Send personalised check-in communications at 30/90/180-day intervals post-completion
•	Monitor Companies House filings for client businesses to detect growth signals, director changes, or risk indicators
•	Generate referral requests at optimal moments (post-completion satisfaction peak)
•	Produce client health scores combining: facility status, engagement, referral activity, and lifetime value
•	Create personalised refinancing proposals when market conditions improve for existing clients
Key Metrics
•	Client retention rate: 70%+ repeat business within 24 months
•	Referral generation: 1 referral per 3 completed deals
•	Renewal capture rate: 60%+ of expiring facilities
•	Net Promoter Score: 8+
3.6 The Compliance Agent — “Regulator”
Attribute	Detail
Mission	Ensure all operations comply with FCA requirements, data protection law, and anti-money laundering regulations.
Trigger	Every client interaction, document creation, marketing output, and data processing activity.
Escalation	All — this agent advises and flags, but you make every compliance decision.
Core Capabilities
•	Screen all outreach and marketing content for FCA financial promotions compliance before distribution
•	Run KYC/AML checks on new clients using Companies House, sanctions lists, and PEP databases
•	Maintain an auditable record of all client communications, decisions, and consent
•	Generate and manage GDPR-compliant data processing records, privacy notices, and consent tracking
•	Monitor regulatory updates from the FCA, ICO, and HMRC and flag anything affecting your operations
•	Produce compliance reports for your annual FCA submissions and internal audit trail
Key Metrics
•	100% of marketing material reviewed before publication
•	KYC completion: < 4 hours for standard checks
•	Zero data breaches or GDPR violations
•	Audit trail completeness: 100%

6. Implementation Roadmap
A phased rollout that gets you operational quickly while building towards full automation. Each phase is designed to generate revenue before moving to the next.
Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
Goal: First deal flow with minimal agent infrastructure.
•	Deploy the Sales Agent with your existing Prospector Stack data
•	Configure outreach sequences targeting your cleaned dataset of 8,500+ historical lending contacts
•	Build the Deal Processing Agent with Companies House integration (using the Firebase proxy we just built)
•	Set up Firestore data models for leads, deals, clients, and audit logs
•	Establish your lender panel and commission agreements
•	Revenue target: First 2–3 deals submitted to lenders by end of month 1.
Phase 2: Growth Engine (Weeks 5–8)
Goal: Automated lead generation and deal processing at scale.
•	Deploy the Marketing Agent with LinkedIn content automation and email nurture sequences
•	Launch targeted Google Ads campaigns for high-intent commercial finance keywords
•	Integrate the Accounts Agent with Xero for automated invoicing on deal completion
•	Build the command centre dashboard for real-time pipeline and agent performance monitoring
•	Refine agent handoff protocols based on Phase 1 learnings
•	Revenue target: 5–10 deals in pipeline, 2–4 completions per month.
Phase 3: Retention & Compliance (Weeks 9–12)
Goal: Client lifecycle management and regulatory robustness.
•	Deploy the Client Relationship Agent with automated renewal tracking and referral generation
•	Deploy the Compliance Agent with FCA financial promotions review and KYC automation
•	Implement full audit trail across all agent activities
•	Create client portal for document submission and deal progress tracking
•	Establish monthly agent performance review cycle
•	Revenue target: Consistent 5+ completions per month with 20%+ from referrals/renewals.
Phase 4: Scale & Optimise (Month 4+)
Goal: Maximise throughput and commission income per deal.
•	Introduce specialist vertical agents (e.g., property finance, invoice finance, asset finance) for deeper lender matching
•	Build lender relationship scoring to route deals to lenders with best approval history
•	Implement predictive analytics on pipeline conversion and seasonal patterns
•	Explore white-label model: license your agent infrastructure to other brokers (the original Veltro vision, proven by your own usage)
•	Revenue target: 10+ completions per month, £10K+ monthly commission, sub-2% operational cost ratio.

8. Risk Management
Risk	Severity	Mitigation
Agent hallucination in client comms	HIGH	All external communications require your approval for first 90 days. Gradually release based on accuracy metrics. Never auto-send financial advice.
FCA compliance breach	HIGH	Compliance Agent reviews all marketing. You approve all regulated content. Maintain manual oversight of financial promotions.
Data breach / GDPR violation	HIGH	Encrypt all PII at rest and in transit. Firestore security rules. Regular access audits. Data retention policies.
Single point of failure (you)	MEDIUM	Document all processes. Agents can continue routine operations if you are unavailable for 24–48 hours. Critical decisions queue for your return.
API downtime / rate limits	MEDIUM	Graceful degradation. Queue failed requests for retry. Multiple LLM provider fallback (Gemini → Claude → GPT).
Lender relationship damage	MEDIUM	You personally manage all lender relationships. Agents prepare submissions but you review before sending to key lenders.
Email deliverability issues	LOW	Proper domain warmup. Multiple sending domains. Monitor blacklists. Stay under 100 emails/day/domain.
 
9. Your Daily Operating Rhythm
Here is what a typical day looks like when your agents are fully operational. Your active work time should be 3–5 hours per day, focused entirely on high-value activities.
Time	Activity	Detail
08:00	Morning Briefing	Review overnight agent activity digest: new leads, deal updates, escalations, financial alerts.
08:30	Approve Queue	Review and approve/edit outbound communications, marketing content, and submissions flagged by agents.
09:00	Client Calls	Take discovery calls and relationship meetings booked by the Sales Agent. Pre-briefed with full prospect dossier.
11:00	Deal Review	Review credit packs prepared by the Deal Processing Agent. Approve submissions, adjust lender targeting.
12:00	Lender Liaison	Follow up on live deals with lenders. This is relationship work that requires your personal touch.
13:00	Strategy / Build	Optimise agent prompts, refine workflows, add new lenders to panel, develop new products.
15:00	Afternoon Check	Quick review of agent dashboards. Handle any afternoon escalations. Agents continue working autonomously.
17:00	End of Day	Agents generate EOD summary. You review tomorrow’s priorities. Agents continue nurture sequences overnight.
